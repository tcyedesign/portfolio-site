What is Cloudflare Animation

What is Cloudflare? is a flagship video introducing the Cloudflare brand and service to introduce the brand to B2B and B2C audiences. It is the first video project strategized and created in-house. In collaboration with creative leadership, I defined the visual style and crafted the style frames and that set the visual foundation, partnering with an external agency to bring the animation to life.
