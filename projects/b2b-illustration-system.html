B2B Illustration System

Partnered with the Creative Director to explore and evolve Cloudflare’s B2B illustration style—bringing depth, airiness, and sophistication into our visual language while keeping its simple geometric foundation.

Designed to scale seamlessly from product dashboards and blog posts to digital campaigns and physical event spaces, the system is built on a library of reusable, modular components. This allows designers to easily assemble and custom-puzzle elements together to create dynamic hero illustration or lightweight spot artwork in minutes.

Elevating Brand Expression for Enterprise

While Cloudflare’s legacy illustration style was functional and clear, it was constrained by a narrow color palette and simple flat geometry that lacked the visual depth needed for enterprise-grade B2B customers.

To achieve our business goal of driving enterprise growth while meeting internal demand for a more versatile visual language, we evolved our visual identity into a richer, more sophisticated system—infusing depth, airiness, and dynamic elements while maintaining a strong geometric foundation.
